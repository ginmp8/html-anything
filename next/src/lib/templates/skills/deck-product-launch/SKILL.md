# Product Launch Keynote
Use a cinematic dark cover and light interior slides, with warm orange-to-peach accent color.
- Cover: clear product headline and launch identity.
- Explain the customer problem and why the product was built.
- Introduce the named product with a focal hero visual when available.
- Present the genuine features, followed by pricing information only if supplied.
- Close with an actionable CTA and availability statement based on confirmed launch details.
- Keep the presentation content-led; never invent release dates, features, prices or claims.
