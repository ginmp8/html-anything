# Investor Pitch Deck
Use a clean white base with blue-purple accents and a decisive fundraising narrative.
- Typical content: cover (logo, tagline, funding round and ask); problem; solution; why now; product; market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM); traction; business model; go-to-market; team; funding ask; contact.
- The requested amount and traction must come from the user; never invent revenue, valuation, customers or market size.
- Show bars only for supported numerical series. Use a large dedicated ask slide when funding details are available.
- The structure is a reusable outline, not a mandatory ten-slide limit. Adapt to the available evidence and user-supplied scope.
